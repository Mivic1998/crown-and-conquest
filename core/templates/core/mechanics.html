How the Realm Works

Crown & Conquest is a strategy game about balancing power, prosperity, public order, and military ambition. The exact formulas are hidden, but the principles below explain how your decisions shape the fate of your realm.

Core Strategy

Your kingdom changes over time based on policies, resources, stability, happiness, events, and war. Strong rulers do not simply maximise one value. They balance competing needs.

  • High taxes raise income but can damage happiness.
  • Agriculture supports food production and famine resilience.
  • Infrastructure improves long-term efficiency and economic strength.
  • Military investment strengthens your army but may leave the realm underfunded elsewhere.
  • Welfare helps happiness and stability, especially during difficult periods.

Turns

Each turn represents a period of rule. When you end a turn, your current policies are processed and your kingdom changes.

  • Your resources may rise or fall.
  • Your population, treasury, food, happiness, and stability may change.
  • Your army may grow or improve depending on investment.
  • A turn report is created so you can review what happened.
  • Random events may occur after a turn.

Free rulers receive a limited number of turns per day. Premium rulers receive additional daily turns.

Policies

Policies are your main tool for shaping the kingdom. They do not produce isolated effects; they interact with the wider condition of the realm.

Tax Rate

Taxes increase treasury income, but excessive taxation can reduce public happiness and weaken long-term stability.

Agriculture Investment

Agriculture improves food production. Low agricultural investment can be dangerous if population is high or food stores are already low.

Infrastructure Investment

Infrastructure supports long-term efficiency, trade, logistics, and general economic resilience.

Military Investment

Military spending helps increase army strength and quality. However, focusing too heavily on war may weaken food, welfare, or infrastructure.

Welfare Investment

Welfare supports citizens and can improve happiness and stability. It is especially useful when taxes are high or the realm is under pressure.

Food, Happiness, and Stability

These three systems are closely connected.

  • Food shortages can damage happiness and stability.
  • Low happiness makes the kingdom more fragile.
  • Low stability increases the danger of unrest and crisis.
  • A large army can help maintain order, but cannot replace good governance.

Events and Crises

Events represent major problems such as famine, unrest, rebellion, desertion, or economic disruption.

When an event occurs, you must write a royal response. The AI council evaluates your response on qualities such as empathy, leadership, and practicality.

Strong responses can reduce the damage caused by a crisis. Weak, vague, cruel, or unrealistic responses may fail to protect the kingdom.

Important: The AI does not directly control the game. It provides scores and feedback. The backend simulation applies the final effects.

AI Evaluation

AI is used to judge written player responses. It rewards thoughtful, specific, realistic answers and penalises vague or lazy writing.

For event responses, the council considers:

  • Does the response address the actual crisis?
  • Is it practical with the kingdom's resources?
  • Does it show leadership?
  • Does it consider the people affected?
  • Does it avoid empty promises?

For rallying cries, the council considers:

  • Does the speech inspire the army?
  • Does it show command and purpose?
  • Does it acknowledge danger or sacrifice?
  • Does it offer direction rather than empty glory?

War

War is resolved through a single decisive battle in the current MVP. There are no sieges, alliances, peace negotiations, or territory transfers yet.

Battle outcomes are influenced by:

  • Army size
  • Army quality
  • Rallying cry evaluation
  • Hidden momentum
  • Prestige
  • A small random factor

The exact result cannot be calculated perfectly by players. This preserves uncertainty and prevents war from becoming a simple comparison of army numbers.

Momentum and Prestige

Momentum and prestige are hidden strategic forces.

Momentum

Winning battles can improve a kingdom's confidence and battlefield rhythm. Losing battles can damage it. Momentum is not shown directly, but recent performance may offer clues.

Prestige

Prestige rewards rulers who fight worthy opponents. Repeatedly attacking much weaker kingdoms is discouraged and may harm long-term reputation.

These systems help prevent farming and make war more strategic.

Battle Reports

After a battle, both rulers receive a battle report showing the result, casualties, rallying cries, and council feedback.

Reports are designed to be immersive. They explain what happened without revealing every hidden modifier.

Premium Features

Premium features provide convenience and visual customisation, not unfair battle advantages.

  • Additional daily turns
  • Premium realm badge
  • Custom banner colour

Premium does not directly improve army strength, rallying cry scores, AI evaluations, or battle outcomes.

Strategic Advice

The best rulers study their reports and adapt over time.

  • If food is low, prioritise agriculture.
  • If happiness is falling, reduce pressure or invest in welfare.
  • If stability is weak, avoid reckless taxation and war.
  • If preparing for battle, invest in military strength before declaring war.
  • If your kingdom suffers repeated crises, rebalance instead of doubling down.

What Is Hidden?

Some values are intentionally hidden to preserve uncertainty.

  • Exact event calculations
  • Exact battle strength calculations
  • Random battle factor
  • Precise momentum value
  • Precise prestige value

You are given enough information to make strategic choices, but not enough to solve the game with perfect certainty.

Future Features

The current game focuses on the core kingdom loop and single-battle wars. Future versions may add deeper diplomacy, alliances, peace offers, sieges, territory transfer, espionage, and multi-turn wars.
